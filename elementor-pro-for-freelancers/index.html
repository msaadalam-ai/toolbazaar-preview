Can I order Pro for a client's site before it goes live?

Yes. Activation works on a staging or temporary domain. If the final domain is different, tell us and we move the activation once during the year.

What if my client stops paying me?

Ask us on WhatsApp. The activation is per website for 12 months; what happens to the site itself is between you and your client.

Do I need the client's hosting login?

No. We only need a temporary WordPress admin login from the Temporary Login Without Password plugin, which expires on its own.

Can I use Pro on my own portfolio site?

Yes. Your own site counts like any other: ₨799 a year, or ₨599 each once you are at ten or more sites in total.
