Why a landing page needs Pro

The free plugin already gives you the blank Canvas template and the drag-and-drop editor, so you can lay out a page. What it lacks is the part that makes the page earn money: a form. Elementor Pro's Form widget collects the lead and then does something with it, such as emailing you, redirecting to WhatsApp with a pre-filled message, or sending the entry to a webhook that drops it into a Google Sheet or CRM. Pro also adds popups, motion effects and a Custom Code screen for the tracking pixels your ads need.

Where the leads go

  • Email. Every submission to your inbox, with the fields you choose.
  • WhatsApp. Redirect to a wa.me link after submit so the lead opens a chat with you immediately.
  • Sheet or CRM. A webhook action posts the entry to an automation service that writes it to a sheet or your CRM.
  • Email tools. Built-in integrations with common newsletter services.

Tracking conversions

Paste the Meta Pixel and Google tag into Elementor Pro's Custom Code screen once, set to load on every page. Send the form to a thank-you page and count views of that page as conversions in your ad platform, or fire the event on submit. Keep the page itself lean: one font, compressed images, no sliders, five or six sections at most. A slow landing page wastes ad money before anyone reads it.

Elementor Pro for landing pages: questions

Still unsure? Message us on WhatsApp.

Can I run A/B tests with Elementor Pro?

Not built in. Make two pages and split the traffic from your ad platform, or use a separate testing tool.

Can a form send leads straight into WhatsApp?

A form can redirect to a WhatsApp link after submit and email you the details. Pushing the entry itself into a WhatsApp chat needs an automation service or plugin.

Does Pro include hosting or a domain?

No. You need your own WordPress hosting and domain; Pro is the plugin that builds the pages.

How fast can I get a landing page live?

Activation is usually within about an hour during 10am–10pm PKT. How fast the page itself is ready depends on you.
