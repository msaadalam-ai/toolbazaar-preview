What a school website has to do

Parents come for three things: admissions, notices and fees. Then they look for the staff, the campus photos and a phone number. A school site built on free Elementor can show all of that as static pages, but it cannot take an admission enquiry, and every notice means calling the designer. Pro adds the form, and with the Posts widget the office can publish a notice from the WordPress dashboard and it appears on the home page by itself.

Pro features that fit a school

  • Forms with file upload. Parents attach a B-form or previous result card; the office receives an email. Collect only what you need for the enquiry and keep the rest for the visit.
  • Posts widget and Loop Grid. Notices, results announcements and events as normal WordPress posts, shown automatically.
  • Theme Builder. A header with the school's phone, admissions button and a top bar for urgent notices.
  • Role Manager. An office account that can add notices but not break the design.
  • Downloads. Fee challans, syllabus and date sheets as PDFs in the media library, linked from the page.

Keeping it alive after launch

Most school websites die six months after launch because nobody updates them. Two things help: give the office a limited login so posting a notice is a two-minute job, and decide who renews Elementor Pro each year (we remind you 15 days before). If the school would rather not manage WordPress at all, Pro + Care at ₨2,999 a year includes monthly backups, plugin and theme updates and small fixes on request.

Elementor Pro for schools: questions

Still unsure? Message us on WhatsApp.

Can parents submit admission forms online?

Yes. Elementor Pro's form supports file upload, so a parent can attach documents and the office receives them by email. Ask only for what the enquiry needs.

Can the office update notices without breaking the design?

Yes. Notices are ordinary WordPress posts shown by the Posts widget, and the Role Manager can limit the office account to content only.

Can we show results or downloadable PDFs?

Yes. Upload PDFs to the media library and link them. A results portal with student logins is a separate plugin, not part of Elementor Pro.

What does Pro + Care include?

Everything in the Elementor Pro plan plus monthly backups, plugin and theme updates and small fixes on request, for ₨2,999 a year.
