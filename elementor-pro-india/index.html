India has one of the largest WordPress communities in the world, and Elementor is the page builder most freelancers there learn first. The free plugin is enough to start, but the moment a client wants a custom header, a lead form or a WooCommerce checkout, the project needs Pro.

Can AI Web Tools accept Indian rupees or UPI?

No. We take Pakistani rupees through bank transfer, JazzCash and Easypaisa. UPI, Paytm and Indian bank transfers do not reach Pakistan. Ask on WhatsApp whether a route exists for you before you pay anything.

Does the activation work on a site hosted in India?

Yes. Where the site is hosted does not matter. We need a WordPress site with the free Elementor plugin installed, a temporary admin login, and a server that can reach Elementor's servers for updates.

What hours can I reach you from India?

We work 10am–10pm Pakistan time, which is 10:30am–10:30pm IST. Messages outside those hours are answered the next working morning.

Visitors will not. Inside WordPress, the Elementor license screen shows Pro as active and may show the account it is connected to.
