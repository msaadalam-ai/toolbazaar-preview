How Elementor Pro activation works, and exactly what AI Web Tools does on your site

Activation is a five-minute job once the pieces are in place. This page explains the pieces, so you know what you are handing over and what you get back.

Short answer

Elementor Pro is activated by connecting a WordPress site to an Elementor account that holds a Pro subscription. Once connected, the site can download the Pro plugin, receive updates and import Pro templates. AI Web Tools does this on your site under its own agency subscription: you pay ₨799, share a temporary admin login, and we install and connect Pro, usually within about an hour during 10am–10pm PKT. The temporary login expires on its own afterwards.

What activation does, technically

Elementor Pro checks its license by talking to Elementor's servers. When a site is connected to an account with a valid subscription, Elementor's servers answer "active" and allow three things: the Pro plugin download itself, update notices in your WordPress dashboard, and imports from the Pro template library. Activation does not change your theme, pages or settings; it is a license check plus an update channel.

Our process, step by step

  1. You have a WordPress site with the free Elementor plugin installed. Any host, any theme.
  2. You pay ₨799 by JazzCash, Easypaisa, Raast or bank transfer and send the screenshot on WhatsApp with your site address.
  3. You install "Temporary Login Without Password" from the WordPress plugin directory, create a link with the Administrator role and a 24-hour expiry, and send it to us.
  4. We log in through that link, install the Elementor Pro plugin, connect it to our subscription, confirm the license screen shows active, and open the editor once to check the Pro widgets appear.
  5. We send you a screenshot of the active license on WhatsApp. The activation date is noted for your renewal reminder.
  6. You delete the temporary login or let it expire. We cannot log in again after that.

What we touch, and what we do not

  • We install one plugin, Elementor Pro, and connect it. That is the whole job.
  • We do not change your theme, pages, menus, users, settings or other plugins.
  • We do not need your hosting panel, FTP, database or email login, and we will not ask for them.
  • We do not open WooCommerce orders, form entries or user lists. The work happens on the Elementor license screen and one editor session.

How updates reach your site

WordPress checks for plugin updates on its own schedule. With the activation in place, Elementor's servers tell your site when a new Pro version exists and it shows under Dashboard › Updates like any other plugin. You click update; nothing goes through us. This works for 12 months from activation, after which you renew for another year.

Common hiccups and how we handle them

  • The host blocks outbound connections. A few cheap hosts firewall calls to Elementor's servers; the license then shows as unable to connect. We tell you, you ask the host to allow it, and we try again.
  • A nulled Pro copy is already installed. It must be deleted first, and we recommend a malware scan. A genuine activation cannot "clean" a cracked copy.
  • An old PHP or WordPress version. Elementor publishes minimum versions; if your site is below them, your host can usually switch PHP in a minute.
  • The temporary link expired. Create a new one; it takes thirty seconds.

If we cannot activate Pro on your site at all, you get a full refund.

Questions people ask

Still unsure? Message us on WhatsApp.

Why do you need admin access at all?

Installing a plugin and connecting a license happens inside WordPress admin. The temporary login limits that access to one role and a short window, and it expires on its own.

Can I give you a normal admin user instead?

You can, but the temporary link is better for you: it expires by itself and you do not have to remember to delete an account afterwards.

Can I do the connection step myself?

No. The activation is tied to our Elementor account, so the connect step has to be done from our side. Everything before and after it is in your hands.

Will you see my customers' data?

We work only on the Elementor license screen and one editor session to verify the widgets. We do not open orders, users or settings, and the login expires.
