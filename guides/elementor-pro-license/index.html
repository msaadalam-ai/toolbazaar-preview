What an Elementor Pro license includes, and what an AI Web Tools activation includes

Two different things are sold under the name 'Elementor Pro'. This page sets them side by side so you can pick the one that fits you.

Updated 20 October 2026 · Written by AI Web Tools · How we write guides

Short answer

An Elementor Pro license is a yearly subscription tied to an Elementor account. It gives you the Pro plugin, updates, the Pro template library and Elementor's support for one or more websites depending on the tier, for as long as you keep paying. An AI Web Tools activation gives you the Pro plugin, its updates and the template library on one website for 12 months, under AI Web Tools' own agency subscription, for ₨799. You do not get your own Elementor account, license key or Elementor support tickets.

What Elementor sells

Elementor sells Pro as a subscription in tiers. The tiers differ mainly in how many websites you can activate, from one site on the Essential plan at $60 a year up to agency tiers covering hundreds of sites. Every tier includes the same Pro plugin, updates for the subscription period, the Pro template kits and access to Elementor's support. Current tiers and prices are on Elementor's pricing page; we only quote the single-site figure here because it is the one most people compare against.

What 'activation' means

Installing the Pro plugin is not enough; it has to be connected to an Elementor account that holds a valid subscription. The connection registers your site against that account. From then on the site can download Pro updates from Elementor, import Pro template kits, and show the license as active. Remove the connection, or let the subscription lapse, and the plugin keeps working but stops updating and loses the template library.

Why the difference matters

Because the activation runs under our subscription, your updates depend on that subscription staying active; your pages do not. If you must own the license yourself, for example because your company's procurement rules say so, or you want to open support tickets with Elementor, or you want to manage ten sites from your own account, buy from Elementor. If you want Pro running on a site at a rupee price with someone on WhatsApp who can fix an activation problem, AI Web Tools fits. Neither option gives you a modified plugin; the file comes from Elementor's servers in both cases.

Things neither option includes

  • Hosting, a domain or a theme. Elementor Pro is a plugin; everything around it is yours to arrange.
  • Elementor's hosting product or its AI credits, which are separate purchases.
  • Third-party add-ons, even ones built for Elementor.

Questions people ask

Still unsure? Message us on WhatsApp.

Is the plugin you install the same file as from Elementor?

Yes. Pro is downloaded from Elementor's servers through the activation. We do not modify it, and it updates from Elementor like any other licensed copy.

Can I later move to my own license?

Yes. Buy a license from Elementor, disconnect our activation and connect yours. Your pages, templates and settings stay exactly as they are.

What happens at the end of the 12 months?

We remind you 15 days before. Renew for ₨799 and nothing changes on the site. If you do not renew, Pro stays installed and your pages keep working, but updates and the Pro template library stop until it is activated again.

Do you give me the license key?

No. The activation is tied to AI Web Tools' own subscription, which is what makes the ₨799 price possible.
