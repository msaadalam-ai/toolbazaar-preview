Short answer

Elementor Pro's Essential plan costs $60 a year for one website when bought from Elementor. At ₨280 per US dollar that is about ₨16,800, before the currency-conversion fee and tax that most Pakistani banks add to foreign card payments. AI Web Tools activates the same Pro plugin on one website for ₨799 a year, paid by JazzCash, Easypaisa, Raast or bank transfer.

Where the ₨16,800 comes from

Elementor lists the Essential plan at $60 a year for one website. We convert at ₨280 per dollar, which gives ₨16,800. The real amount on your card statement is usually higher: Pakistani banks add a currency-conversion margin, and many charge tax on international card spending. We do not publish those charges because they differ by bank and change often; check your own bank's schedule of charges. Elementor's dollar price can also change, so the pricing page is the source of truth.

Why Pakistani buyers pay more than the sticker price

  • Most debit cards are blocked for international online payments until you enable them in the bank's app, and some banks do not allow it at all.
  • The bank's exchange rate is worse than the interbank rate you see in the news.
  • Tax on foreign card spending is added by many banks.
  • Renewals are recurring foreign charges, which some cards decline a year later, so the site silently drops back to the free plugin.

Neither Elementor's price nor ours includes hosting, a domain, a theme, Elementor's AI credits or Elementor's hosting product. An AI Web Tools activation also does not include your own Elementor account, license key or Elementor support tickets; this page lays out the difference.

You get the genuine plugin and updates from Elementor; what you give up is a license in your own name. That trade is right for most freelancers and small businesses in Pakistan and wrong for companies that must own every license, and we say so on every page.
