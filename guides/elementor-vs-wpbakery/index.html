Short answer

WPBakery is an older builder, often bundled with premium themes, sold as a one-time license at $82; it leaves shortcodes in your content if you switch away. Beaver Builder is a stable, developer-friendly builder from $89 a year with cleaner output and a smaller community. Elementor Pro costs $60 a year from Elementor or ₨799 a year through AI Web Tools, has the largest community, and is what most Pakistani clients ask for.

If you inherited a WPBakery site

Keep it running if it works, update the bundled version through the theme, and plan a rebuild when the design needs a refresh. Switching builders always means rebuilding layouts; budget for it rather than hoping for a converter.

Picking for a new site

For most freelancers and small businesses in Pakistan, Elementor is the practical choice because of the tutorials, talent and client familiarity. See Elementor vs Divi vs Bricks for the other two big options.
