Paying ToolBazaar ₨799 for an Elementor Pro activation by JazzCash, Easypaisa, Raast or bank transfer, and what to send on WhatsApp afterwards.

Short answer

Message ToolBazaar on WhatsApp with your website address, ask for the current account details, then send ₨799 (or ₨599 per site for 10 or more) by JazzCash, Easypaisa, Raast or bank transfer. Send a screenshot of the payment on the same WhatsApp chat. After that you share a temporary login and we activate Pro, usually within about an hour during 10am–10pm PKT.

Step by step

  1. Message us on WhatsApp with your site address and how many sites you need.
  2. We reply with the account details for your chosen method.
  3. Pay the exact amount and keep the transaction ID.
  4. Send the screenshot showing the amount, date and transaction ID.
  5. Create a temporary login and send the link. See temporary login safety.
  6. We activate and send a screenshot of the active license.

Good habits when paying anyone online

  • Only pay to account details given in the chat you started from our website's WhatsApp button.
  • Check the account name before confirming the transfer.
  • Never share your PIN, OTP or card number with anyone, including us. We never ask for them.

If something goes wrong

If we cannot activate Pro on your site, you get a full refund. If you sent the wrong amount, tell us on WhatsApp and we will sort it out before activation. More in refunds and renewals.
