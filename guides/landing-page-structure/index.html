A landing page structure that converts

A simple landing page structure for ads and offers: one goal, headline, proof, offer, form and FAQ, built with Elementor Pro. Section by section.

Updated 20 October 2026 · Written by ToolBazaar

Order on WhatsAppSee pricing
Short answer

A landing page that converts has one goal and every section supports it: a headline that states the offer, a short line on who it is for, three concrete benefits, proof such as photos, numbers or reviews, the offer with price, a form or WhatsApp button, answers to the usual objections, and the call to action repeated at the end. Remove the main menu so visitors cannot wander off.

Section by section

  1. Hero: headline with the offer, one supporting line, the button, and a real photo.
  2. Who it is for: one or two lines so the right people stay.
  3. Three benefits: outcomes, not features.
  4. Proof: photos of real work, numbers you can back up, genuine reviews with permission.
  5. Offer: what they get and what it costs.
  6. Form or WhatsApp: as few fields as possible.
  7. FAQ: delivery, payment, refunds, timing.
  8. Final call to action.

Building it in Elementor

Use the Canvas page template to hide the theme header and footer, the Pro Form widget for leads, and Custom Code for your ad platform's tag. See form to WhatsApp and adding a pixel. The full use-case page is Elementor Pro for landing pages.

Honest claims only

Do not invent reviews, countdowns that reset, or "only 2 left" when there are 200. Ad platforms reject pages for misleading claims, and customers remember. Say what is true and make it easy to act on.

Questions people ask

Still unsure? Message us on WhatsApp.

How long should a landing page be?

Long enough to answer the questions that stop people from acting. For a cheap, simple offer that is short; for an expensive one it is longer.

Should a landing page have a menu?

Usually not. A menu gives visitors exits that do not lead to your goal.

Form or WhatsApp button?

Both can work. A form gives you a record; WhatsApp gives speed. Many pages offer the form and redirect to WhatsApp after submit.
